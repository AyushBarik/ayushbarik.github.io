About Me

I (wish to) revolutionize the world with cheap and powerful Generative AI. My work focuses on "Efficient Diffusion" for Image and Audio. Particularly, I have worked in Efficient Algorithms ([2]) and Efficient Systems ([3], [4]). I do research under Prof. Fan Lai. I plan to pursue a PhD. I like to watch anime/manga, read webnovels, play chess, invest.
